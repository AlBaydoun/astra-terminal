## What changed

- One fixed M15 strategy shared by the chart, paper runner and split study: EMA20/100 trend/pullback, ADX14, RSI14, preceding-20-bar tick activity and ATR14 stop/target distances. Uses exactly 250 completed candles. No forming-candle vote or future-price input.
- Main-chart green ASTRA BUY and red ASTRA SELL markers, aligned EMA lines, a plain-language WAIT checklist, current setup entry/SL/TP references and a direct chart shortcut. The shortcut opens M15 candles and collapses the lower panel for chart space. BOTS returns to controls. Mini charts do not offer this main-chart-only indicator.
- On the later request to restore Hammer/Engulfing labels, added an always-visible Chart labels selector: Candlestick Patterns, ASTRA Confluence (15m), Both indicators, None. It uses the existing `astra_ind` preferences and stays synchronized with the Indicators menu. The bot's chart shortcut now preserves enabled pattern labels. These are display choices; switching them does not change the scanner or bot strategy. Candlestick Patterns was restored as the user's saved choice.
- Corrected the outer grid's automatic minimum width, which had widened the 1280px terminal to about 1860px and hidden controls. The toolbar scrolls horizontally, the chart/sidebar fit the window, and the bot settings wrap inside their panel.
- New paper bot with Start, Pause, Scan all pairs now, chart shortcut and a guide/results link. It receives ranked setups from the dedicated Confluence Scanner. Fixed M15 signals; on Al's subsequent request, execution counts are editable and default to no count limit. A single scan can fill multiple eligible pairs; new signals can add positions in the same pair that day.
- Trading rules form controls total open positions, open positions per pair, related positions, entries per pair per broker day, per-trade position value, risk, total allocation and daily loss budget. Zero in the first four fields means no count limit. Maximum trade counts fills those four fields with zero without raising percentage budgets. Save applies the form atomically; Reset defaults loads values without erasing trades. Controls and unfinished input survive automatic refresh. Default per-trade allocation is 5% of equity, risk 0.5%, total allocation 100%, daily budget 2%; broker minimum lots may exceed that allocation. Percentage budgets remain finite; total allocation cannot exceed 100% of equity.
- Dedicated Confluence Scanner page automatically covers all 271 instruments currently reported by JustMarkets. Six concurrent candle requests, eight-second cancellation, evaluations cached until the next M15 candle, readiness refreshed every 15 seconds. Search and filter controls stay alive during automatic updates. Exact pairs, BUY/SELL/WAIT, checks passed, state, rejection/data reasons, SL/TP and Chart buttons are visible.
- Candidate selection uses lower estimated round-trip cost relative to ATR, then higher ADX. A rejected candidate does not prevent the next eligible candidate from being chosen. Pausing the scanner pauses new entries; saved position protection continues. Failed data clears old signals. Scanner errors are visible per instrument, not silently treated as no signal.
- Existing risk/spec/price/permission checks apply at each final execution, reserving earlier fills before sizing another pair. New 90-second expiry and 0.25 ATR price-drift guard prevent chasing. At most 12 bars held. Saved stops are managed while new entries are paused. Daily loss locks are saved even when no fill occurs, preventing reload/recovery from discarding a reached lock.
- Dedicated paper dispatch never reaches the live arming/submission route. Web Locks serialize this paper account across tabs; saved settings/history are reread before entry. A failed save retains the in-memory position and blocks further entries.
- Ordinary new-bot keys `astra_botcfg_confluence` (also scanner preference and the new `confluenceLimits` preferences) and `astra_bot_confluence`; indicator selection remains in existing `astra_ind`. Freeform trade metadata holds the broker day, signal bar, initial entry fee and risk. Only this new bot adds `confluenceEntries` counters inside its existing daily records, saved with each fill. These preserve configurable daily counts and prevent duplicate fills across refreshes, tabs, closes, restarts and the engine's 500-close retention. Old records remain readable; other bots' ledgers are untouched. The Confluence page computes fully costed closed P&L/R without rewriting stored trades.
- Fixed research uses the earlier eight-market snapshot, with a separate adapter around the existing audited intraday simulator. The earlier candidate code/data/results are unchanged. This candidate is excluded from automatic Market Fit sweeps/retuning.

## Limits

Only 14 gold trades, not 100 new forward trades. The independent eight-market study is not a backtest of the subsequently requested ranked full-catalogue portfolio, multiple positions, repeat entries or editable allocations. No signal thresholds or costs were tuned to improve results. Costs unchanged/worse; other/stock commission profiles remain prior assumptions, not new live-account evidence. No claimed most-profitable strategy. Past labels are setups, not fills. Risk, quote, permission, lot-size and account-currency-conversion limits can reject a labelled setup. Paper exits require the PC, ASTRA and bridge running. Forward drawdown samples available quotes and inherits the 1,500-point curve retention; terminal-wide reports retain their earlier accounting convention. The new bot's own page and the study include both fees.
