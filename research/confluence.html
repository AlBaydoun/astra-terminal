ASTRA Confluence

A patient M15 indicator and paper bot. Five checks must agree. This is an experiment, not established as profitable.

Choose the labels on your chart

The Chart labels selector above the chart switches between Candlestick Patterns (Hammer, Bullish Engulfing and other familiar patterns), ASTRA Confluence (15m), Both indicators or None. Your choice is saved. Opening a Confluence chart preserves pattern labels if they are enabled. Display choices do not start, stop or change the bot's strategy.

When to switch it on

Open Bots → Confluence Scanner to see automatic scans of every connected JustMarkets instrument. It checks new completed M15 candles and refreshes readiness every 15 seconds. Open ASTRA Confluence → Start paper bot to let the bot select eligible pairs. It waits for setups during 11:00–17:45 broker time. Use any row's Chart button to see its exact pair and indicator.

Green ASTRA BUY: an upward trend recovers its EMA20, with ADX strength, RSI momentum and tick activity agreeing. Red ASTRA SELL: the reverse, proposing a short. Labels appear after the candle closes. Grey WAIT: do nothing. The five-check score is not a win probability.

Stop: 2 ATR. Target: 4 ATR. Hold: at most 3 hours. The bot can open several eligible pairs in one scan and repeat a pair on a new completed-candle signal. Open ASTRA Confluence to set total positions, positions per pair, related positions and daily entries per pair. 0 means no count limit; Maximum trade counts puts 0 in these four boxes. Press Save trading rules to apply. Reset defaults fills the original controls without erasing history.

Trade counts default to no count limit. Each new position defaults to at most 5% of equity, leaving funds for other trades, with at most 0.5% risk. All open positions share a 100% position-value budget and a 2% daily loss budget. These percentages are editable; total position value stays at or below 100% of equity. A 5% allocation can be too small for a broker's minimum lot, especially gold and FX. The scanner explains any refusal. Higher counts do not guarantee more signals or profit.

The bot prefers lower estimated trading costs relative to ATR, then higher ADX; it skips any candidate refused by risk checks. It refuses stale prices, expired setups, excessive price drift, prohibited pairs and unaffordable broker lots. A refresh or restart cannot fill the same signal again. Labels show setups; only the paper ledger records actual simulated entries.

Keep the PC, ASTRA, MT5 and the bridge running for paper exits. Saved stops return after restart but cannot execute while the PC is off. The new bot has no broker-order route.
